Why Cabinet Painting is the Smart Choice for Ottawa Homes

When your cabinets are structurally sound, cabinet painting offers a cost-effective, efficient alternative to replacement. If you're already updating your space, many homeowners pair cabinet refinishing with interior painting for a more complete refresh.

Eco-Friendly Renovation: Refinishing keeps perfectly good cabinet boxes out of landfills and uses low-VOC paints to protect your family and pets.

Increased Home Value: A refreshed kitchen is a proven way to boost your home's appeal in Ottawa's competitive real estate market.

Our Cabinet Painting Process

  1. 1

    In-Home Assessment & Quote

    We evaluate your cabinet material, condition, and any needed repairs. You receive a detailed, transparent quote with timeline and material recommendations.

  2. 2

    Preparation & Setup

    We remove and label all doors, drawers, and hardware, then deep clean, sand, fill imperfections, apply bonding primer, and protect surrounding areas.

  3. 3

    Professional Paint Application

    Multiple thin coats of premium cabinet-grade coatings are applied with light sanding between layers for proper adhesion and a smooth, uniform surface.

  4. 4

    Drying & Curing

    We allow adequate drying time between coats, working within optimal conditions. Light use is typically fine within a few days; full curing takes approximately 2–3 weeks.

  5. 5

    Final Inspection & Cleanup

    Every surface undergoes a thorough quality check. We touch up any imperfections, reinstall hardware with precise alignment, and leave your kitchen clean and ready for use.
