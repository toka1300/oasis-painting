Built For Ottawa Weather

Choosing the Right Paint for Ottawa's Climate

Not every paint is right for exterior painting Ottawa homes need. Ottawa's climate includes freeze-thaw cycles, high humidity, UV exposure, and temperature swings. That is why choosing the right product matters.

For many surfaces, high-performance 100% acrylic latex paint is a strong choice because it expands and contracts better than lower-quality options. For masonry or stucco, elastomeric paints can help bridge hairline cracks caused by freeze-thaw movement. Satin and low-lustre finishes are often recommended because they repel moisture while avoiding the overly shiny look that can highlight imperfections.

Lighter neutral colours can also perform better over time because they usually fade less under UV exposure. For areas affected by humidity, paints with antimicrobial properties may help protect the surface.

Our team helps you choose materials that fit your house, surface type, style preferences, and budget.

The cost of exterior painting services in Ottawa usually ranges from $2 to $6 per square foot, depending on the size of the property, surface condition, number of coats, paint quality, access, prep work, and project complexity.

  • How much should it cost to paint the exterior of a 2,000 square foot house?

    For a 2,000 square foot house, exterior painting can often range from approximately $5,000 to $12,000, depending on the surface area, type of surface, and prep required. A simple project in good condition may cost less, while a complex house with peeling paint, damaged surfaces, multiple colours, or difficult access may cost more.

  • How much does it cost to stain a house in Ottawa?

    House staining costs in Ottawa vary by home size, surface material, preparation level, and the previous products used. Compared to painting, staining can be more variable because the existing coating and condition of the surface can significantly affect the prep process. Ottawa Exterior Maintenance

How to Protect Your Home's Exterior in Ottawa

Ottawa weather can be tough on exterior paint and siding. Seasonal maintenance and early repairs can help extend the life of your home's exterior surfaces and reduce long-term damage.

  • Know When to Repaint

    Fading, peeling, cracking, or exposed surfaces are common signs that exterior paint may need attention.

  • Watch for Moisture Damage

    Ottawa snow, rain, and humidity can lead to moisture intrusion, wood rot, and paint failure if surfaces are not maintained properly.

  • Inspect Caulking Regularly

    Cracked or failing caulking around windows, trim, and doors can allow water penetration behind painted surfaces.

  • Protect Wood Siding

    Wood siding should be inspected regularly for peeling paint, cracking, and moisture exposure to help prevent long-term deterioration.

  • Clean Exterior Surfaces

    Removing dirt, mildew, algae, and buildup can help exterior coatings last longer and maintain appearance.

  • Address Peeling Paint Early

    Small peeling areas can spread quickly when exposed to Ottawa freeze-thaw cycles and seasonal weather changes.

Pro Tip

Regular exterior inspections and maintenance can help extend paint lifespan and reduce larger repair costs over time.
